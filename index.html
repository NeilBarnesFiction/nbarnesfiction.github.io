The Primal Kid

In Hampton Roads, Virginia, Karla Sayer is rebuilding a life marred by tragedy, reclaiming her hard-earned strength as a powerlifter while enjoying her uninhibited sexuality. Around her, Grace Benitez, Jacquelyn Mercer, and David Scott are each confronting questions of trust, responsibility, faith, and connection.

As relationships change and long-buried truths emerge, something extraordinary begins to intrude on their ordinary lives.

The Primal Kid is a character-driven speculative novel about love, sexuality, faith, resilience, and the difficult work of remaining connected to yourself and to other people.

For mature readers. Contains explicit sexual content.
